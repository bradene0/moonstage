## Reading it

**The fixes shrank the output, not the effort.** Single files halved, the tower's source dropped by a third
and its follow-up edit by 30% (compact JSON). The tower agent finished 30% faster. But tool calls stayed
in the same 40–50 range, and the dodger agent spent more: both agents put the time they saved into
verification, inspecting headless runs and renders between edits.

**Physics was still the biggest trap.** The run-2 tower stood in its tests but collapsed after 60–90 s on
default settings: warm starting alone slowed the creep without stopping it. Moonstage at `36a0586` adds
body sleeping and feature-keyed contacts; both run-1 and run-2 towers now move about 1 px in 3 minutes,
and the regression test covers 180 s plus waking a stack when a post is knocked out.

So Moonstage is still slower than freestyle for one-shot briefs. The gap is now verification the agents
chose to do, not friction they had to work around.

## Friction the agents hit (fixed after this run)

1. **Stacks still crept** over minutes (above). Fixed with sleeping.
2. **`time()` kept counting across scene switches**, though the docs say it counts from the scene start.
   Fixed.
3. **Unset declared state read as `undefined` in tests**, while behaviors read 0. Now 0.
4. **No way to apply setup to `run`/`render`**, and `--input-script` would not take a test file. Both
   flags now accept a test file, and `--setup` takes edit operations.
5. **Overlapping bodies at scene start** were silent and exploded on the first tick. `MS2019` warns.
6. **`build --out` resolved against the project**, unlike `pack --out`. Now relative to the current
   directory.
7. **Reading another entity's state** was undocumented (the name must be in your own `state` list). Now in
   the MoonBit primer.
